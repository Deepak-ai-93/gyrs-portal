How Gujarat Rojgar Setu Works

A simple, user-friendly 4-step path enabling candidates to easily understand, apply, and track their entire employment lifecycle.

01
Create Free Profile

Register candidate profile with educational qualifications, certified skills, and district. Instant Aadhaar & student verification.

Aadhaar Verified
02
Explore & 1-Click Apply

Filter verified government and corporate openings across all 33 districts. Apply instantly in 1-Click or via official direct recruitment portal.

Fast-Track Apply
03
Track Application Status

Monitor your live application lifecycle in the Candidate Dashboard: Applied ➔ Screening ➔ Shortlisted ➔ Interview Call.

Real-Time Tracker
04
Rozgar Mela & Offer

Attend scheduled interview calls, document verification, or state Rozgar Melas and receive your official verified appointment letter.
